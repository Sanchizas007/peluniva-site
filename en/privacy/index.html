Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

In short

Peluniva has no account and no sign-up. It does not collect your name, e-mail address, health information or location, and it has no advertising or analytics of its own. Your training history stays on your phone.

What stays on your phone

Your goal, plan, completed sessions, reminder times, language and sound settings are saved in a small file inside the app on your device. We cannot see it and it is not sent to us. Deleting the app, or choosing "Erase my data" in Profile, removes it.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple. We never see your card or payment details. To confirm that a subscription is active and to understand how the app is doing, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription service. It receives an anonymous app user ID and the purchase information from the App Store (for example which plan was bought and when). It is not linked to your name or e-mail address and is not used for tracking.

Apple Ads attribution

If you found Peluniva through an Apple Ads campaign, the app can send Apple's attribution token, once per install, through RevenueCat. Apple answers which campaign led to the install. The token does not identify you. Peluniva does not read the advertising identifier and does not show the App Tracking Transparency prompt.

Reminders

Reminders are local notifications made by your phone at the times you choose. There is no push server, and no notification token is sent to us. You can turn them off at any time in Profile or in iOS Settings.

Children

Peluniva is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your rights and contact

Because we keep no personal data about you, there is usually nothing to access or delete. If you have a question or a request about the data that RevenueCat holds for your anonymous ID, write to us and we will help you.

We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version.
